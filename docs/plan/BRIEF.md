# Essence redo: brief

Agreed with Youssef on 2026-11-12.

## What it is
A full rebuild of the Essence portfolio demo: a storefront for an invented fragrance and skincare house called Essence. It is a front-end demo; nothing can really be bought.

## Scope
- Whole rebuild: new design, structure and copy. The product data shape (JSON catalogue, sizes/variants, cart) can be reused where it fits.
- The old streetwear catalogue goes. Its real brand names (Asili, Kush) and their product photos are removed; every product is invented, with licensed photos (Pexels/Unsplash) or our own renders.
- No backend: static site, cart in localStorage, mock checkout.

## Audience
In between: good enough to win Fiverr/Upwork buyers who want a shop like this, but with a design-led look that a brand with taste would also respond to. Distinctive, not experimental at the cost of usability.

## Languages
English and Spanish (kept from the current site).

## Must-do for a visitor
Browse the collection, open a product, choose a size/variant, add to bag, and reach a believable (fake) checkout.

## Product and logo
- Range: 12 products, 6 fragrances and 6 skincare (agreed).
- Name stays Essence; the logo gets redrawn in the design phase (agreed).
